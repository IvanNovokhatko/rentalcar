## Rental Car

Rental Car is a modern application that allows you to find a rental car that
will perfectly fit your needs and style.

## Main Functional

- Look for your perfect car in the catalog. Use the link "Catalog" in the header
  or the button "View Catalog" to see it yourself.
- Use filters to find the car that will perfectly suit your needs and budget.
- Choose your perfect car from the catalog by clicking "Read More".
- Fill up a simple rent form and click "Send". Our team will soon contact you at
  your email!

## Getting Started

First, run the development server:

```bash
npm run dev
# or
yarn dev
# or
pnpm dev
# or
bun dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000] (http://localhost:3000) with your browser to see
the result.

You can start editing the page by modifying `app/page.tsx`. The page
auto-updates as you edit the file.
